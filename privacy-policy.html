Privacy Policy – Nursery App

ZmrAppStudio · Effective date: November 8, 2026

ZmrAppStudio built the Nursery App as a free app for children. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page explains how information is handled when you use the app. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information Collection and Use

Nursery App does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the app asks for no personal details. The app works offline. Your language choice (English or Hindi) is saved only on your own device and is never sent to us.

Sound / Text-to-Speech

The app speaks words using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. We do not record, collect or store any audio or voice data.

Advertising and Analytics

The app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first and ads will only come from Google Play Families-compliant sources.

Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect standard store and device information under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies.

Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for young children, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or from anyone else. We do not share, sell, rent or transfer children’s personal information. Parents and guardians stay in control. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will take the necessary action.

Security

Because the app does not collect your data, there is nothing stored on our side. We still work to keep the app safe, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Links to Other Sites

The menu options Update, Rate, Share and Other App open Google Play or other external pages. We do not control those sites and advise you to read their privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
