Privacy Policy – Nursery App

ZmrAppStudio · Effective date: November 9, 2026

ZmrAppStudio built the Nursery App as a free educational app for young children. This page explains what the app does, what information may be handled, and how children’s privacy is protected. By using the app you agree to this policy.

About the App (WebView Container)

Nursery App is a WebView container: it displays web content inside the app. The page it opens is set by us through a link stored in Firebase, so we can update the learning content without releasing a new version. If no link is set, the app opens the built-in learning content that ships with the app. Because the app loads this content from the internet, an internet connection may be used while the app opens. We choose the content that is loaded and do not knowingly load pages that collect personal information from children.

Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase services: Firebase Realtime Database (to read the link of the content to display) and Firebase Analytics. Firebase may automatically collect limited technical and usage data such as app launches, app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate region (from IP address), crash and diagnostic reports, and an app-instance identifier. This data is used only to keep the app working, fix problems and understand overall usage. It is not used for advertising, profiling or personalised content. Firebase is operated by Google; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Personal Information

The app has no sign-up or login and does not ask for a name, email, phone number, photo, location, contacts or any other personal detail. No personal information is collected from children without verifiable parental consent. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer children’s personal information. Your language choice (English or Hindi) and the last opened link are stored only on your device.

Sound / Text-to-Speech

The app speaks words using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. We do not record or store any audio or voice.

Advertising

The app does not show ads. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated first and only ad providers allowed for children’s apps (Google Play Families policy) will be used.

Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for young children, including children under 13. We comply with applicable children’s privacy rules such as COPPA and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that personal information of a child was collected without parental consent, we will delete it. Parents and guardians who believe this may have happened can contact us and we will take the necessary action.

Cookies and Local Storage

The app does not use advertising cookies. Content shown in the WebView may use local storage on your device to remember the settings mentioned above.

Security

We use commercially reasonable means to protect the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Links to Other Sites

The menu options Update, Rate, Share and Other App open Google Play or other external pages. We do not control those sites; please read their privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.
